A warning of noise on CS on the Diagnostics tab, and a page that copes better with a weak Wi-Fi link.

**What changed:**
- **A warning of noise on CS.** The Diagnostics tab now compares each boot's CS count with a clean boot's: 224 or 225 plus the payload's transfers. A higher count means noise on the IPL chip's select line, usually from the wiring. An extra transfer early in the read makes the console start its own menu. "This boot" says how many transfers too many, and the boot log marks such boots, including those logged before the update.
- **Steadier status updates on a weak link.** The page asks for the status one request at a time, and waits longer after failures: 10, 20, then 30 seconds, and every 5 seconds again once a request gets through. Before, requests that never finished piled up on the Pico, and it could take minutes to answer again once the link was good.
- **Wiring advice in the README:** wires of at most about 10 cm, of 26–28 AWG stranded wire, as upstream PicoBoot asks, and a flex cable or a quick-solder board at U10 as the better choice. On one console, loose wires picked up enough noise at its TV to make most boots fail, though they had been clean at the bench.

The injection, your payloads, the settings and the command-line tool work as before.

**Updating from 1.1.x or 1.0.x:** install `picoboot-wifi-1.1.2-update.bin` on the Firmware tab, or with `python3 picoboot-wifi.py update picoboot-wifi-1.1.2-update.bin`. It starts on trial, and it keeps itself after the next power-on that boots the console. A first install or a recovery uses the UF2, as the [README](https://github.com/r3mko/PicoBoot-WiFi#readme) describes.

**Files:**

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `picoboot-wifi-1.1.2.uf2` | The USB install: a first install, an upgrade from PicoBoot-DX, or a recovery. Copy it onto the Pico's drive (hold BOOTSEL while connecting USB). It leaves the payloads in place |
| `picoboot-wifi-1.1.2-update.bin` | The update over Wi-Fi from 1.1.x or 1.0.x (the Firmware tab, or the CLI's `update`) |
| `picoboot-wifi.py` | The command-line tool (Python 3, standard library only), the same as in 1.1.0 |
| `SHA256SUMS` | The SHA-256 of each file |

**Notes:** as for 1.0.0, except for `.dol` files (see 1.1.0). Change the default Wi-Fi password (`picoboot`). Tested on two DOL-001s.
